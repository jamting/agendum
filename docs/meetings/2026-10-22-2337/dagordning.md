### § 5 Should building a feature become a skill of its own, `/feature`?

Today the steps for building a feature are a long passage of prose in CLAUDE.md. Claude reads it every time and follows it from memory, so the order sometimes drifts and a small step gets forgotten, such as a line in SCREENS.md or a box's F1 text. The suggestion is a `/feature` skill: the same steps as a short checklist, done in the same order every time (habits first, Agenda's evidence, test, core, TUI, the documents, the gate and the long fuzz run, commit). The owner asks the panel to advise on three questions: (a) is the skill wanted at all, or is the prose enough; (b) should the skill replace the prose, or should CLAUDE.md keep the rules and the skill only give the order, leaving two places to keep in step; (c) should a small check go with it, a command listing the documents a change has left untouched?
