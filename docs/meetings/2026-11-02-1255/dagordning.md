## Items to discuss

### § 5 A better note editor for Markdown

The owner wants the editor to help with Markdown: headings, lists and to-do lists, and links to other pages; more will surely come. Today a note is plain text on screen (R64e), and Markdown becomes HTML only on the site. Links between notes exist already as Zettel links (R73b).

### § 6 A post's metadata

The owner wants a publishing date (like Things' start date, generally useful), a modified date on all items set automatically, a slug for public links (blog posts at `/year/month/day-slug.html`, configurable), and a title that can override the item's text. Today a post's date is its When date, else its Entry date, its address its Zettel id, and its title its text.

### § 7 Templates edited outside Agendum, with assets

Templates for the start page, a blog's index and detail pages, and other pages, kept outside Agendum with their assets. Could the menu be one template? Today the site's HTML is made in code, and only `style.css` can be changed.

### § 8 Other pages, such as /about and /now

The ability to add pages that are not posts. Today the site has the index, the posts and the feeds only.

### § 9 Several feeds from one Agendum file

Multiple sources of feeds in one Agendum file. Today a file has one blog with one Posts category and one pair of feeds.

### § 10 The Publish settings need rework

The settings need rework. Today one box holds thirteen fields: what is published, about the site, where it goes and how it is uploaded, all kept in the file.

### § 11 Maybe: a third-party static publisher?

Should Agendum use a third-party static site generator instead of growing its own?

### § 12 Maybe: dogfooding

Build `https://jamting.github.io/agendum/` with the publisher. Today `mix agendum.pages` assembles it, and the devlog there is already published by the blog extra.

### § 13 The publisher's roadmap: the order

What comes first, from the items decided above.
