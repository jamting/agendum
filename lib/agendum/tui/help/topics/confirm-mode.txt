Confirm Mode

PURPOSE       Whether Agendum asks "are you sure?" before doing
              something that is hard to take back.

STEPS         F10 Utilities Customize, Confirm mode: Yes (the
              default) or No.

NOTE          With No, discarding items, categories and views,
              deleting macros, sorting a family and the like happen
              at once. CTRL-Z still undoes them. Questions whose
              answers do different things are always asked.
