Reposition Names in a List

PURPOSE       Put the views, categories or macros in the order you
              want.

STEPS         Highlight the name, then ALT-↑ or ALT-↓ moves it up
              or down: in the **View Manager**, the **Macro
              Manager**, and among a category's siblings in the
              **Category Manager**. ALT-F5 sorts a list by name
              instead.
