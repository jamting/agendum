Views

PURPOSE       A view is one way of looking at your file: a page of
              sections, each showing the items of one category. An
              item can turn up in many views, the way Pooh turns up
              wherever there is honey.

IN A VIEW     Section head    A category, its items listed below
              Item            Anything you typed: a task, an idea,
                              a phone number
              Column          More categories beside each item:
                              its date, who it is for, how urgent

EXAMPLES      * A view called Calls: the calls to make, and in a
                column whom to call.
              * A view called This Week: a section for each day,
                with what falls on it.
              * A view called Friends: a section each for Piglet,
                Rabbit and Eeyore, with what you promised them.

KEYS          F8 (VW MGR)     Choose another view, or add one
              F6 (PROPS)      Change the highlighted section or
                              item
              F10 (MENU)      The **View Menu**: the view's commands
