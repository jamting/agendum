Automatic Completion

PURPOSE       Type the start of a category's name and let Agendum
              finish it, in a column or a box's category field.

STEPS         1. Type the first letters. The first category that
                 matches is shown after them.
              2. F8 or ALT-N offers the next match, F7 or ALT-P the
                 one before; F3 lists them all.
              3. ENTER takes the one shown.

NOTE          To make a new category of exactly what you typed,
              press F5 or INS (CTRL-N) instead of ENTER.
