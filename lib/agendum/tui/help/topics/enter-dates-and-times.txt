Enter Dates and Times

PURPOSE       Type dates as you would say them; Agendum works out
              the day.

WHERE         * In an item's text: the first date becomes its When
                date ("Call Kanga next Tuesday").
              * In a date column, or with ALT-F2 (WHEN) for the
                highlighted item.
              * In a date filter, a datebook view, a schedule.

WHAT YOU      today, tomorrow, friday, next week, this month
CAN TYPE      november 20, 11/20, 2026-11-20
              3pm, 15:30, noon, morning, tonight, in 2 hours
              every monday, every 2 weeks, the 15th of each month

NOTE          A weekday alone is its next one (today, if today). A
              date without a year is in the future. A time without
              am or pm falls in the day: "3:30" is 3:30pm. Global
              date settings (File Properties) set the week, the
              year and the times of morning and evening.

              **Global Date Settings Box**
