Show a Schedule for Any Day

PURPOSE       List one day's items in the Show View: what is on for
              Thursday.

STEPS         1. F10 Utilities Show Sched.
              2. Date: type it, or F3 for the calendar (today at
                 first).
              3. Date category (When) and Filter if you like, then
                 ENTER.

NOTE          ALT-B then browses to the days before and after.

SEE ALSO      **Schedule Box**  **Create a Show View**
              **Browse Through Dates**
INDEX         Schedule > Show; Dates > Schedule; Show View > Schedule
