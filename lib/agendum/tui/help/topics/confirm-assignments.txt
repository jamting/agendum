Confirm Assignments

PURPOSE       Let Agendum suggest the categories an item's text
              matches, and choose yourself which it gets.

STEPS         1. File Properties, Auto-assign settings, Confirm
                 assignments: Always, or Sometimes (only partial
                 matches are asked about).
              2. A ? shows at the top while suggestions wait.
              3. F10 Utilities Questions: for each item, SPACE picks
                 a category, TAB all of them, ENTER assigns them and
                 goes on; ESC stops.

NOTE          Never (the default) assigns every match at once. A
              category can set its own Confirm assignments in its
              Advanced settings.
