Conditions

PURPOSE       A condition lets a category take items by itself:
              Urgent takes every item in both Taxes and Accountant;
              Me takes every item in no person's category.

HOW THEY      * A condition names another category: items in it, or
WORK            not in it, or with a date or number in a range.
              * Every condition must hold, except that children of
                an exclusive family are alternatives (High or Low).
              * Items are checked when they are typed, edited or
                assigned; an item that stops meeting the conditions
                leaves the category again.
              * New conditions reach items already in the file only
                when executed: F10 Utilities Execute, or ALT-X.
