Discard a Category

PURPOSE       Take a category, and its children, out of the file.
              The items stay; only their assignments to it go.

STEPS         1. Highlight it in the **Category Manager**, or its
                 section head in a view.
              2. Press ALT-F4 (or DEL in the Category Manager).
              3. Press ENTER at the question.

NOTE          A discarded category does not go to the trash: only
              CTRL-Z brings it back. Sections and columns it headed
              go from every view. MAIN, Entry, When and Done cannot
              be discarded.
