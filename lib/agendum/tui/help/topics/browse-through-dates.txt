Browse Through Dates

PURPOSE       Turn the pages of a datebook view, or move a view's
              date filter, to earlier or later dates.

STEPS         1. Press ALT-B (BROWSE). BROWSE shows at the top
                 right.
              2. ← / → a period earlier or later (in a datebook,
                 its period; with a filter, a day). With a filter,
                 ↑ / ↓ also go a week, PGUP / PGDN a month,
                 CTRL-PGUP / CTRL-PGDN a year. ALT-F5 goes to a
                 typed date.
              3. ENTER keeps the new dates, ESC goes back.

NOTE          In a standard view, browsing needs a date filter on
              the section or the view. The dates kept are fixed
              ones: "today" becomes the date it was browsed to.
