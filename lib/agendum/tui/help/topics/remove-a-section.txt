Remove a Section

PURPOSE       Take a section out of the view. The category and its
              items stay in the file and in other views.

STEPS         1. Highlight the section's head.
              2. Press DEL (or F10 View Section Remove).

NOTE          CTRL-Z puts the section back. To get rid of the
              category itself, discard it: see **Discard a
              Category**.

SEE ALSO      **Add a Section**  **Sections**
INDEX         Sections > Remove; Remove > Sections
