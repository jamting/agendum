Category Manager

PURPOSE       The whole hierarchy on one screen: every category
              under MAIN, where you add, rename, move and discard
              them. F9 opens it from a view; F9 or ESC goes back.

KEYS          INS or CTRL-N   Add a category below (ALT-U above,
                              ALT-R a child, ALT-L after the parent)
              F2              Rename it
              F5              Its note
              F6              Its properties: type, exclusive
                              children, conditions and actions
              F7 / F8         Promote / demote it
              ALT-↑ / ALT-↓   Move it among its siblings
              ALT-F10         Move it under another parent
              ALT-F5          Sort its family by name
              DEL or ALT-F4   Discard it and its children
              ALT-− / ALT-=   Collapse / expand families
