Numeric Columns

PURPOSE       Keep a number with each item, such as hours or costs,
              and let the section add them up.

STEPS         1. Add a column with Category type Numeric (or make a
                 category numeric in its properties).
              2. Type a number in the column beside an item: signs,
                 a currency or % symbol and thousands separators are
                 fine.

TOTALS        In the column's properties choose the format and the
              calculations: Count, Total, Average, Minimum, Maximum.
              They show under the section.

NOTE          A category made numeric cannot change back.

              **Numeric Functions Box**
