Enter Categories

PURPOSE       Add a category to the hierarchy.

STEPS         In the **Category Manager** (F9):
              1. Highlight the category the new one goes beside.
              2. Press INS (or CTRL-N) to add one below it, ALT-U
                 above it, ALT-R as its child, or ALT-L after its
                 parent.
              3. Type the name and press ENTER.

ELSEWHERE     * Typing a new name in a column makes it a child of
                the column's head.
              * ALT-D or ALT-U adds a section to a view; a new name
                there makes the category too.
