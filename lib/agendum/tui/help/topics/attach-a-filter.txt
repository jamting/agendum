Attach a Filter

PURPOSE       Show only the items in (or not in) some categories.

STEPS         1. F10 View Properties (the whole view), or F6 on a
                 section head (one section).
              2. Filter: press SPACE to open the category list.
              3. Highlight a category; SPACE once shows items in
                 it (+), twice items not in it (-), three times
                 neither.
              4. ENTER or ESC closes the list, keeping it.

SEE ALSO      **Filters**  **Change a Filter**  **View Properties Box**
INDEX         Filters > Attach; Views > Filter; Sections > Filter
