Starter Applications

PURPOSE       A ready-made file to start from, instead of an empty
              one.

THE PLANNER   The Activities Planner (planner.agendum, in Agendum's
              starters folder) has categories for people, projects,
              tasks, activities, priorities and expenses, with views
              to match, and no items. Copy it, retrieve the copy,
              and type your own.
