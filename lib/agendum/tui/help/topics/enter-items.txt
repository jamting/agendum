Enter Items

PURPOSE       An item is anything you want to keep: "Ask Owl how to
              spell Tuesday", "Three pots of honey by Friday". Type
              it as it comes to you; Agendum files it.

STEPS         1. Highlight the section the item belongs in.
              2. Start typing (or press INS or CTRL-N first).
              3. Press ENTER to keep it, or ESC to let it go.

WHAT          The item goes in the section's category, and in every
HAPPENS       category whose name its text holds: "Take Rabbit his
              umbrella" goes to Rabbit too, if you have a Rabbit.
              The first date in the text ("by Friday", "next
              Tuesday at 3pm") becomes its When date.

TIPS          * F5 while typing keeps the text and opens the item's
                note, for what does not fit on one line.
              * F2 edits the highlighted item later; F4 marks it
                done.
