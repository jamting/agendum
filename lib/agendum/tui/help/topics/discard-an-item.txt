Discard an Item

PURPOSE       Take an item out of the file altogether, from every
              section and view. It goes to the trash, where you can
              get it back for a while.

STEPS         1. Highlight the item (or mark several with F7).
              2. Press ALT-F4 (DISCARD), or CTRL-D.
              3. With items marked, choose Marked items or Current
                 item.
              4. Press ENTER at "Discard this item?" (ESC keeps it).

NOTE          To take an item out of one section only, and keep it
              everywhere else, use DEL instead: see **Break an
              Item's Assignment**. CTRL-Z undoes a discard.

SEE ALSO      **Undiscard an Item**  **Mark or Unmark Items**
              **File Properties Box**
