Actions

PURPOSE       An action happens when an item joins a category: the
              item is also put in another category, taken out of
              one, given a date or a number, marked done or
              discarded.

HOW THEY      * They run once, when the item joins: by your hand, a
WORK            condition or another action.
              * A parent's actions run for items that join its
                children as well.
              * What an action assigns stays, even when the item
                leaves the category again.
