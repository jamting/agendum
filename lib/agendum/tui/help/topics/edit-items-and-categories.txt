Edit Items and Categories

PURPOSE       Change what an item says, or rename a category, where
              you see it.

STEPS         1. Highlight the item, the section head, or the
                 column entry.
              2. Press F2 (EDIT), or CTRL-E.
              3. Change the text, then press ENTER (ESC leaves it as
                 it was).

NOTE          An edited item is filed again by what its text now
              says: a category name or a date you add takes effect.
              Renaming a section head or a column entry renames the
              category everywhere.
              In the **Category Manager** F2 renames the highlighted
              category. MAIN, Entry, When and Done keep their names.
