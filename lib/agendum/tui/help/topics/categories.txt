Categories

PURPOSE       A category is a name you file items under: a person, a
              project, a kind of task, a priority. Sections and
              columns show categories, and the items in them.

ABOUT         * Categories form one hierarchy under MAIN: Rabbit can
CATEGORIES      be under Friends, under People.
              * An item in a category is also in every category
                above it: in Rabbit, it is in Friends and People
                too.
              * A category whose name appears in an item's text
                takes the item by itself.
              * Entry, When and Done are date categories: they hold
                the item's dates.
