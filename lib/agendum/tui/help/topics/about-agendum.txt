About Agendum

PURPOSE       Wol's Agendum is a free-form information manager in
              the manner of Lotus Agenda (1988): you type things as
              they come, and categories, views and conditions put
              them in order.

THE IDEA      * Type first, organise later: an item finds its own
                categories from its words and dates.
              * One item, many places: it shows in every section of
                every view whose category it is in.
              * Views are ways of looking, not folders: make as many
                as you like.
