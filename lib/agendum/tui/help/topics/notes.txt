Notes

PURPOSE       A note is longer text kept with an item or a category:
              the details of a task, a person's address, minutes of
              a meeting.

ABOUT NOTES   * An item or category with a note shows a ♪ in the
                control panel when it is highlighted.
              * F5 (NOTE) opens the note of what is highlighted.
              * Search (ALT-F6) can look in notes as well as items.
