Attach a Numeric Filter

PURPOSE       Show only the items with a number in a range: costs
              over 100, hours under 2.

STEPS         1. In a filter's category list, choose a numeric
                 category with SPACE.
              2. In the box: Show items Assigned (or Not assigned),
                 Minimum and Maximum, and Inside or Outside the
                 range.
              3. ENTER.

SEE ALSO      **Range Filter Box**  **Filters**  **Numeric Columns**
INDEX         Filters > Numeric; Numeric > Filter
