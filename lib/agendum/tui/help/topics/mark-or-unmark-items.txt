Mark or Unmark Items

PURPOSE       Mark several items so that one command acts on all of
              them: discard, mark done, move, copy, assign.

STEPS         * F7 (MARK) marks the highlighted item, or unmarks it.
                A marked item shows a diamond.
              * F7 on a section head marks every item in it (again:
                unmarks them).
              * ALT-J goes to the next marked item.
              * ALT-F7 unmarks every item.

NOTE          With items marked, a command that can act on many asks
              first: Marked items, or Current item (the highlighted
              one). The control panel shows a diamond while any item
              is marked.
