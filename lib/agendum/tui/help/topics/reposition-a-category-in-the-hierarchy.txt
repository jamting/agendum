Reposition a Category in the Hierarchy

PURPOSE       Move a category, with its children and its items, to
              another place in the hierarchy.

STEPS         In the **Category Manager** (F9), highlight it, then:
              F7 (PROMOTE)    Up a level, after its parent
              F8 (DEMOTE)     Down a level, under the one above
              ALT-↑ / ALT-↓   Up or down among its siblings
              ALT-F10 (MOVE)  Under another parent you choose

NOTE          If a move would put an item in two children of an
              exclusive family, Agendum settles that first, so no
              item ends up in both.
