Copy Conditions/Actions

PURPOSE       Give one category the conditions or actions another
              has: Barney takes the items Fred takes.

STEPS         In the **Category Manager**:
              1. Highlight the category to copy from.
              2. Press ALT-F9 (CPY C/A).
              3. Highlight the category to copy to, then ENTER (ESC
                 gives it up).
              4. If it has both, choose Condition & action,
                 Condition or Action, then ENTER.

RESULT        What is copied takes the place of the target's
              conditions or actions; the rest stays. The source does
              not change. Execute (ALT-E) to apply it to items
              already in the file.

NOTE          ALT-F8 (CLR C/A) clears the highlighted category's
              conditions or actions, asking first.

SEE ALSO      **Conditions**  **Actions**
              **Clear a Filter, Condition, or Action**
INDEX         Conditions > Copy; Actions > Copy; Copy > Conditions; Conditions > Clear all; Actions > Clear all
