View Types

PURPOSE       What kinds of view there are.

KINDS         Standard    Sections headed by categories you choose
              Datebook    A section for each hour, day, week or
                          month of a period, like an appointment
                          book
              Show View   Made by Utilities Show (matches, done
                          items, alarms, a day's schedule …); the
                          next Show replaces it
