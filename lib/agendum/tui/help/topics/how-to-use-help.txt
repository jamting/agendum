How to Use Help

PURPOSE       Quick answers about Agendum, wherever you are, as Owl
              would give them if he were briefer.

STEPS         1. Press F1 for help on what you are doing now.
              2. Use the keys below to look further while in help.
              3. Press F9 or ESC to leave help and go back to work.

LOOK          PRESS              TO DO THIS
FOR INFO      -----------------  ----------------------------------
              Arrows, HOME, END  Highlight a topic shown in bold;
              then ENTER         ENTER reads it (or type its first
                                 letter to highlight it)
              PGUP, PGDN         Scroll a long topic
              F3 (INDEX)         Choose from an **index|Help Index** of topics
              F5 (FIND)          Type a word to find the topics
                                 with it; ALT-N and ALT-P then
                                 step through them
              F6 (KEYS)          The **keys** and the commands here
              F8 or BACKSPACE    Back through the topics you read
              F9 or ESC          Leave help
