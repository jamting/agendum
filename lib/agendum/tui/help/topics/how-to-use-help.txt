How to Use Help

PURPOSE       Quick answers about Agendum, wherever you are, as Owl
              would give them if he were briefer.

STEPS         1. Press F1 for help on what you are doing now.
              2. Use the keys below to look further while in help.
              3. Press F9 or ESC to leave help and go back to work.

LOOK          PRESS               TO DO THIS
FOR INFO      ------------------  ---------------------------------------
              Arrows, HOME, END   Highlight a topic shown in bold, then
              then ENTER          ENTER to read it (or type its first
                                  letter to highlight it)
              PGUP, PGDN          Scroll a long topic
              F3 (INDEX)          Choose from an **index|Help Index** of every topic
              F5 (FIND)           Type a word to find the topics with it;
                                  ALT-N and ALT-P then step through them
              F6 (KEYS)           The **keys** and the commands where you are
              F8 or BACKSPACE     Go back through the topics you have read
              F9 or ESC           Leave help
