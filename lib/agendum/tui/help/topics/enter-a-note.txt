Enter a Note

PURPOSE       Write a note for an item or a category.

STEPS         1. Highlight the item, or a section head or column
                 entry for its category.
              2. Press F5 (NOTE).
              3. Type. F5 saves the note and goes back; ESC asks
                 first whether to keep the changes.

NOTE          While typing an item, F5 keeps the item's text and
              opens its note. In the **Category Manager**, F5 opens
              the highlighted category's note.
