Assignment Profile for an Item

PURPOSE       See every category an item is in, in the hierarchy,
              and switch them on or off in one place.

STEPS         1. Highlight the item (or mark several with F7).
              2. Press F3 (CHOICES).
              3. Move to a category; SPACE switches it.
              4. ENTER or ESC closes the profile, keeping what you
                 switched.

NOTE          An assignment a condition made shows with a c. ALT-−
              and ALT-= collapse and expand families, as in the
              **Category Manager**.
