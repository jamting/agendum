Attach an Assignment Action to a Category

PURPOSE       Make something happen to every item that joins a
              category.

STEPS         1. F6 on the category in the **Category Manager** (or
                 F10 Category Properties on a section head).
              2. Assignment actions: SPACE opens the category list.
              3. SPACE on a category goes round: assign it, remove
                 it, then off. A date or numeric category opens a
                 box: give items a date ("tomorrow") or a number, or
                 take it away.
              4. Special actions: Mark items done, or Discard items.
              5. ENTER.

NOTE          Category Properties shows the actions: "Sales,
              -Receivables, When=tomorrow".

SEE ALSO      **Actions**  **Category Properties Box**
INDEX         Actions > Attach; Categories > Actions; Special actions
