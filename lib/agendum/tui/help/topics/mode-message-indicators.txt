Mode & Message Indicators

PURPOSE       The word at the right of the top line says what
              Agendum is doing. Nothing shows when it is ready.

WORDS         EDIT      You are typing an item or a name
              AUTOC     A category's name is being completed
              BROWSE    Browsing a view's dates (ALT-B)
              LEARN     Recording a macro
              INS, OVR  Inserting or typing over, in a note
              WRKG      Working in the background

NOTE          Messages ("Item discarded") show on the same line
              until the next key.
