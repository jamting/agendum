Remove a Column

PURPOSE       Take a column out of a section. The category and the
              items' assignments to it stay.

STEPS         1. Highlight the column head (→ or TAB from the
                 section head).
              2. Press DEL (or F10 View Column Remove).
              3. If the column is in other sections too, choose All
                 sections in view or Current section only.

SEE ALSO      **Add a Column**  **Columns**
INDEX         Columns > Remove; Remove > Columns
