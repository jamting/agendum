Value Range Symbols

PURPOSE       How a date or numeric range shows in a filter or a
              condition.

EXAMPLES      [When 2026-11-02..2026-11-06]    Dates from one to
                                               the other
              [Amount 100..]                   100 or more
              [not When ..2026-11-02]          Not up to that date
              if When 2026-11-02..2026-11-06   A condition's range

NOTE          An end left blank is open. "not" means items outside
              the range. Dates show in the file's date format.
