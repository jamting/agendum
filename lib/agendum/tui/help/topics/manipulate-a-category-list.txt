Manipulate a Category List

PURPOSE       Work with the hierarchy lists that boxes open: a
              filter, conditions, actions, the assignment profile.

KEYS          ↑ ↓ PGUP PGDN    Move through the categories
              SPACE            Switch the highlighted one (in a
                               filter: +, then -, then off)
              ALT-− / ALT-=    Collapse / expand families
              ENTER or ESC     Close the list, keeping what you
                               switched
