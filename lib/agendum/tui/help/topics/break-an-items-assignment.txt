Break an Item's Assignment

PURPOSE       Take an item out of one category, and so out of the
              section that shows it, while it stays in the file and
              in its other categories.

STEPS         1. Highlight the item in the section.
              2. Press DEL (or F10 Item BrkAssign).
              3. Press ENTER at "Remove this item from the section?"

              In a column, DEL on an entry takes the item out of
              that category. In the **Item Properties Box** (F6),
              DEL breaks the highlighted assignment.

NOTE          ALT-Y puts the item DEL last took out back into the
              section under the highlight. Use ALT-F4 to discard an
              item from the file instead.
