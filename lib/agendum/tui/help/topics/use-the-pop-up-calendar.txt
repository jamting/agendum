Use the Pop-Up Calendar

PURPOSE       Pick a date from a month's calendar, or just look one
              up.

STEPS         * F3 (CHOICES) in a date column: the calendar on the
                entry's date (or today); ENTER puts the highlighted
                date in.
              * ALT-C anywhere in a view: the calendar, to look.

KEYS          ← → ↑ ↓          A day, a week
              HOME / END       First / last day of the month
              PGUP / PGDN      A month back / ahead
              CTRL-← / CTRL-→  A year back / ahead
              F6               Add a time
              a date typed     Go there
              ESC              Close, changing nothing
