Filters

PURPOSE       A filter shows only some of the items: those in (or
              not in) categories you choose, or with a date or a
              number in a range.

ABOUT         * A view's filter applies to every section; a section
FILTERS         can have one of its own as well.
              * Every condition must hold, except that children of
                an exclusive family are alternatives (High or Low).
              * The filter shows in brackets beside the view's name
                or the section head.
              * An item typed into a filtered view or section gets
                what the filter asks for, so it stays in sight.
