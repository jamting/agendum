Attach an Assignment Condition to a Category

PURPOSE       Make a category take the items that are in (or not in)
              other categories.

STEPS         1. F6 on the category in the **Category Manager** (or
                 F10 Category Properties on a section head).
              2. Assignment conditions: SPACE opens the category
                 list.
              3. SPACE on a category goes round: + items in it, -
                 items not in it, then off. A date or numeric
                 category opens its range box.
              4. ENTER or ESC closes the list; ENTER the box.
              5. ALT-E (or F10 Utilities Execute) to apply it to the
                 items already in the file.

NOTE          Category Properties shows the conditions: "if Taxes,
              not People".

SEE ALSO      **Conditions**  **Attach a Date Condition**
