Items

PURPOSE       An item is one thing you want to keep, in your own
              words: a task, an idea, a phone call to make, a line
              from a book. Agendum files it in categories, and the
              views show it wherever those categories are.

ABOUT ITEMS   * Keep an item's text short, a line or two; what
                needs more room goes in its note (F5).
              * It can be in many categories at once, so it can turn
                up in many sections and views. It is still one item:
                change it anywhere, and it changes everywhere.
              * Every item has the date it was entered (Entry), and
                may have a When date and a Done date.
