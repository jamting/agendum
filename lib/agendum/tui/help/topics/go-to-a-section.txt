Go to a Section

PURPOSE       Jump to a section in a long view.

STEPS         1. Press ALT-F5 (GO TO).
              2. Highlight the section (or type its first letter).
              3. ENTER.

NOTE          CTRL-PGUP and CTRL-PGDN go to the section head above
              or below.

SEE ALSO      **Go To Section Box**  **Sections**
INDEX         Sections > Go to; Go to > Section
