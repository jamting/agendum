Create a Date Category

PURPOSE       Keep another date with items, such as a review date or
              a deadline.

STEPS         1. Make the category (in the **Category Manager**, or
                 as a column head).
              2. F6 on it: Category Properties.
              3. Type: Date. ENTER.

NOTE          Children whose names are dates ("today", "November
              20") become their items' dates; the others are
              dropped, after a question. It cannot change back.
              Show it as a date column to type the dates.

SEE ALSO      **Date Categories**  **Category Properties Box**
INDEX         Dates > Create a category; Categories > Make a date category
