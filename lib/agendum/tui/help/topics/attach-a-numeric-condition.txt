Attach a Numeric Condition

PURPOSE       Make a category take the items whose number is in a
              range: Big Spend takes every cost over 100.

STEPS         1. In a category's Assignment conditions, choose a
                 numeric category with SPACE.
              2. Show items Assigned (or Not assigned), Minimum and
                 Maximum, Inside or Outside.
              3. ENTER.

SEE ALSO      **Attach an Assignment Condition to a Category**
              **Numeric Columns**
