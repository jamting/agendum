Columns

PURPOSE       A column shows, beside each item, which of a
              category's children the item is in: who it is for,
              its priority, its date. Typing in a column assigns the
              item.

KINDS         Standard    The child categories' names (or a star,
                          Y or N, a line of a category note)
              Date        A date, such as When
              Numeric     A number, with totals if you like
              Unindexed   A text of its own: a phone number

NOTE          A column added to all sections is linked: changing
              its width or format changes it in every section.
