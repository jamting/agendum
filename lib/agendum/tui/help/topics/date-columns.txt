Date Columns

PURPOSE       Show an item's date in a column: When, Entry, Done, or
              a date category of your own.

STEPS         * Type a date in the column: "friday", "next tuesday
                3pm", "11/30".
              * F3 in the column opens the calendar to pick one.
              * DEL takes the date away.

FORMAT        A date column's properties (F6) set how it shows
              dates: date and time, day of the week, date format,
              clock.

SEE ALSO      **Columns**  **Calendar Box**  **Display Format Box**
INDEX         Columns > Date; Dates > Columns
