Undiscard an Item

PURPOSE       Changed your mind about something you discarded, as
              Piglet often does? Bring it back, as long as it is
              still in the trash.

STEPS         1. Highlight the item it should follow, or the head
                 of its section.
              2. F10 Item Undisc
              With more than one item in the trash, choose All
              items in trash or Last item in trash in the
              **Undiscard Box**, then press ENTER.

RESULT        The items come back below the highlight, in that
              section's category alone. Their other categories,
              dates and alarms went when they were discarded:
              assign them again where they belong. Conditions still
              run, so some come back by themselves.

NOTE          The trash empties at the end of the day, unless File
              Properties says otherwise (Empty trash). After that
              the items are gone for good. While Agendum is open,
              CTRL-Z undoes a discard too.

SEE ALSO      **Item Menu**  **Utilities Menu**  **File Properties Box**
INDEX         Items > Undiscard; Trash > Recover items; Recover > Discarded items
