About Indicators

PURPOSE       The two lines at the top of the screen tell you what
              is going on.

THE LINES     File line   The file's name (* when it has unsaved
                          changes), a ? while suggested
                          assignments wait (Utilities Questions),
                          a mode word, then the date and time
              View line   The view's name, its filter, and symbols
                          at the right
