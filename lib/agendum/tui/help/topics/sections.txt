Sections

PURPOSE       A section is one part of a view: a category's name as
              its head, and under it the items in that category,
              those in the categories below it too.

ABOUT         * Each section can have its own columns, sorting and
SECTIONS        filter.
              * An item in two of the view's categories shows in
                both sections. It is one item: change it in one, and
                the other shows the change.
              * Typing an item under a section puts it in the
                section's category.
              * ALT-− collapses a section to its head (twice: all of
                them); ALT-= opens it again.
