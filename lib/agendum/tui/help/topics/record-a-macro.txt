Record a Macro

PURPOSE       Teach Agendum a series of keys by pressing them once.

STEPS         1. ALT-F3 opens the **Macro Manager**.
              2. INS (or CTRL-N, or just type) names a new macro.
              3. F7 (LEARN): LEARN shows at the top right. Do the
                 work: every key does its usual job and is recorded.
              4. ALT-F3 stops recording.

PLAY IT       ENTER on the macro in the manager runs it where you
              were; ALT-Z runs the last one again; a key attached in
              its properties (F6) runs it at once.

NOTE          F7 on a macro that has keys asks whether to replace
              them or add to them.

SEE ALSO      **Macros**  **Macro Properties Box**
INDEX         Macros > Record; Learn mode; Macros > Run
