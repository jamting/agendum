Change Properties of a Section

PURPOSE       Set one section's head, sorting, filter and columns.

STEPS         1. Highlight the section's head.
              2. Press F6 (PROPS), or F10 View Section Properties.
              3. Change the settings, then ENTER (ESC puts them all
                 back).

IN THE BOX    Section head    Another category for the section (F3)
              Item sorting    How this section sorts its items
              Filter          Which of its items it shows
              Columns         Its columns, in order: DEL removes
                              one, ALT-↑ / ALT-↓ move one, INS adds

SEE ALSO      **Section Properties Box**  **Sort Items**
              **Attach a Filter**  **Sections**
INDEX         Sections > Properties; Properties > Sections
