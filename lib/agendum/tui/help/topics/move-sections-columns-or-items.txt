Move Sections, Columns, or Items

PURPOSE       Put things where you want them, with ALT-F10 (MOVE).

ITEMS         ALT-F10 on an item: move the highlight to where it
              should go, then ENTER (ESC leaves it). Moved to
              another section, it leaves the old section's category
              for the new one. With items marked, all of them move.

SECTIONS      ALT-F10 on a section head: the sections fold up; ↑ ↓
              move this one, ENTER keeps the new place.

COLUMNS       ALT-F10 on a column head: ← → move the column within
              its section, ENTER keeps it.

NOTE          Moving a section or a column changes only this view.
              ALT-F9 (COPY) puts an item in another section as well,
              instead of moving it.
