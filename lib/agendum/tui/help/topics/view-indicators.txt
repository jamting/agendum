View Indicators

PURPOSE       The symbols at the right of the View line.

SYMBOLS       #    The highlight is on a number or numeric category
              ☼    … on a date or date category
              ⌂    … on an unindexed category's text
              ♪    What is highlighted has a note
              o    An item is caught in circular conditions
              @    An alarm has rung (Utilities Show Alarm)
              ♦    Items are marked

NOTE          The filter, and what the view hides, show in
              brackets after the view's name.
