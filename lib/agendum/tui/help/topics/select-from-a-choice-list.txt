Select from a Choice List

PURPOSE       Pick a setting's value from a list instead of going
              through them with SPACE.

STEPS         1. In a box, highlight the setting.
              2. Press F3 (CHOICES).
              3. Highlight a choice (or type its first letter) and
                 press ENTER. ESC leaves it as it was.

NOTE          SPACE alone goes to the next choice. F8 (DEFAULT) puts
              the setting back to what something new gets.
