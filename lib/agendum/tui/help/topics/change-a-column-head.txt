Change a Column Head

PURPOSE       Show another category in a column, or rename the one
              it shows.

STEPS         * F3 on the column head: choose another category.
              * F2 on the column head: rename its category,
                everywhere.
              * In Column Properties (F6), Column head: type a name
                or press F3.

SEE ALSO      **Modify Column Properties**  **Columns**
INDEX         Columns > Head; Column heads > Change
