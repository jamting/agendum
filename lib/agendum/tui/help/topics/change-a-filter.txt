Change a Filter

PURPOSE       Change which items a view or section shows, or show
              them all again.

STEPS         1. Open the filter's list again: View Properties or
                 Section Properties, Filter.
              2. SPACE on a category goes round: + in it, - not in
                 it, then off.
              3. On a date or numeric category, DEL in its box takes
                 that part of the filter away.

SEE ALSO      **Attach a Filter**  **Filters**
INDEX         Filters > Change; Filters > Remove
