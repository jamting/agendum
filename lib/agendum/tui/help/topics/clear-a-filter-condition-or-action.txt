Clear a Filter, Condition, or Action

PURPOSE       Take a filter, a condition or an action away.

STEPS         * In the list (a filter, conditions or actions), SPACE
                on the category until it shows nothing.
              * In the **Category Manager**, ALT-F8 (CLR C/A)
                clears all of a category's conditions or actions.
              * In a date or numeric filter's or condition's box,
                DEL removes it.

NOTE          A condition taken away lets go of the items it had
              assigned when they are next checked; execute (ALT-E)
              to do it now.

SEE ALSO      **Change a Filter**  **Conditions**  **Actions**
INDEX         Filters > Clear; Conditions > Clear; Actions > Clear
