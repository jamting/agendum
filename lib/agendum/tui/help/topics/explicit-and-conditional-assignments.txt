Explicit and Conditional Assignments

PURPOSE       An item is in a category either because you put it
              there, or because something matched.

KINDS         Explicit      You assigned it (typed, picked, moved),
                            or an action did. It stays until you
                            take it away.
              Conditional   Its text matched, or a condition held.
                            It goes when that is no longer so:
                            "Meet with Pooh" in Meetings, edited to
                            "Telephone Pooh", leaves.

NOTE          The **Item Properties Box** and the assignment profile
              mark conditional assignments with a c.
