Alphabetize Categories

PURPOSE       Put a category's children in order by name.

STEPS         1. In the **Category Manager**, highlight one of the
                 children (or their parent).
              2. Press ALT-F5 (SORT).

NOTE          Only that family is sorted; the families below it keep
              their order. CTRL-Z undoes the sort.

SEE ALSO      **Reposition a Category in the Hierarchy**
INDEX         Categories > Alphabetize; Sort > Categories; Alphabetize > Categories
