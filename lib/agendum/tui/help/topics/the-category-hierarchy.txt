The Category Hierarchy

PURPOSE       All categories hang from MAIN, each under its parent,
              like the branches of a tree: Owl's house in the
              Hundred Acre Wood, as it were.

HOW IT WORKS  * A category's children narrow it: Priority holds
                High, Medium and Low.
              * An item in a child is in the parent too, so a
                section headed by the parent shows it.
              * A family can be exclusive: an item may be in one of
                its children only. The **Category Manager** brackets
                such a family on the left.
              * MAIN, Entry, When and Done are always there.
