Use Child Categories as Section Heads

PURPOSE       Give each child of a category a section of its own:
              a section each for Piglet, Rabbit and Kanga, under
              Friends.

STEPS         1. F10 View Properties.
              2. On Sections, press F3: the Section Select list.
              3. Highlight the parent and press F5 (CHLDRN).
              4. ENTER, and ENTER again.

SEE ALSO      **Add a Section**  **View Properties Box**
INDEX         Sections > Child categories; Children > Sections
