Sort Items

PURPOSE       Keep the items of every section, or of one, in order.

STEPS         1. F10 View Properties (every section), or F6 on a
                 section head (that section).
              2. Item sorting: press SPACE to open the Sort box.
              3. Choose the Primary key, and a Secondary one for
                 ties: item text, a category (its children's order,
                 a date or a number), or a line of a category note.
              4. For each, the order, and where items without a
                 value go; then ENTER.

NOTE          A section can use the view's sorting or its own.
              Items that tie keep the order they were entered in.

SEE ALSO      **Sort Box**  **Sorting**
INDEX         Items > Sort; Sort > Items
