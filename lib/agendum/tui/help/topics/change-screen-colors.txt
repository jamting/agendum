Change Screen Colors

PURPOSE       Choose how Agendum looks.

STEPS         1. F10 Utilities Customize.
              2. Theme: SPACE goes through them (F3 lists them):
                 Agenda's classic colours first, then light and dark
                 ones, and mono for plain terminals.
              3. ENTER.

NOTE          The theme is yours, not the file's: it is kept in
              your preferences for every file.

SEE ALSO      **Customize Box**
INDEX         Colors; Screen > Colors; Theme
