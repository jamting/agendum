Attach a Date Condition

PURPOSE       Make a category take the items whose date is in a
              range: This Week takes everything due this week.

STEPS         1. In a category's Assignment conditions, choose a
                 date category (When, say) with SPACE.
              2. Show items Assigned (or Not assigned: without a
                 date), Start and End date, Inside or Outside.
              3. ENTER.

NOTE          Words like "beginning of this week" are read again
              every day, so items come and go as the days pass.

SEE ALSO      **Attach an Assignment Condition to a Category**
              **Range Filter Box**
INDEX         Conditions > Date; Dates > Conditions
